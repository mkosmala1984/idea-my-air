Session Flyout on the Taskbar Widget

TL;DR A flyout above the widget replaces the tooltip and the menu. It shows three groups: needs input, unread replies, working. A click on a row opens the session and marks it read. The plugin decides “unread” from a per-session “seen” time. Four decisions: what counts as read, which finished sessions show, how the widget shows unread, and the backdrop. Not in scope: autostart, changes to the IDE tool window.
